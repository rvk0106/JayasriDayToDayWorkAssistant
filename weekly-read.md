# Weekly read — the questions Vamsi and the hub agent answer every week

> Vamsi, 2026-11-03: *"There are gaps here — where do we assess? We have to
> write all of this down and answer it every week."* So: one fixed set of
> questions, one dated block per week, answered from the files (`week-plan.md`,
> `updates/`, `metrics.md`, `templates/school-list.md`, `events/`,
> `questions-for-vamsi.md`). The answers feed `progress.md` (the numbers row)
> and next week's `week-plan.md` (the guidance). Nothing here is shown to
> Jayasri as a verdict; it reaches her as the plan.

## The questions

**A. How much, how often**
1. Completion % — tasks ✅ ÷ planned (swaps count as done)
2. Active days — days with a 🌙 Day summary, out of the working days
3. Hours per active day — from the day summaries (start → end)
4. Which days were empty, and was there a reason written?

**B. What slipped, and of what kind**
5. Tasks skipped or carried — list them
6. Pattern: is it one *kind* of task (calls / editing / writing / review) that keeps slipping?
7. Tasks swapped for mood — how many, and did the swap get done?

**C. Are the events on track**
8. Orphanage (Sat Nov 21) — run-up table: which dated items are done, which are late?
9. Schools — list size, contacts made, replies, meetings fixed (`school-list.md`); is the Nov 14 / Nov 28 milestone still reachable?
10. Videos — uploaded this fortnight? (cadence: one every 2 weeks); subscribers reported?

**D. What she raised**
11. `questions-for-vamsi.md` — open rows; which need an answer before next week's plan works?
12. Anything she asked the coach repeatedly (a how-to, a tool problem) — a sign of a missing playbook or tool?

**E. The read**
13. What went well — two specific things
14. Where would she do better — one thing, concrete, next-week sized
15. Plan change for next week — what moves, what is cut, what is added (then write `week-plan.md`)
