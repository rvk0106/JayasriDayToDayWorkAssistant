# Playbook — clip, caption, voice-over, post

The weekly video is the subscriber engine. Cadence beats polish: one video a
week, every week. Vamsi films on the weekend; Jayasri edits 5–10 min a day and posts.

## §1 Cut a 30-second clip (CapCut, 20–30 min)

1. Import the weekend footage. Watch once at 2× speed; note the one moment where the thing *works* (robot moves, lock opens, Yashvi reacts).
2. Trim to that moment: 3 s before, the moment, 3 s after. Target 25–35 s.
3. Crop to 9:16 for Shorts / Reels; keep the subject centred.
4. Remove silence gaps; no music yet.
5. Save the project. Tell the coach "clip cut".

## §2 Caption + voice-over (15–20 min)

1. Write 2–3 Telugu lines that say what is happening and why it matters to a parent. Plain words.
2. Record the voice-over in CapCut (hers or Yashvi's). One take is enough; redo only if a word is missing.
3. Auto-captions → correct the Telugu spelling → font large, bottom third.
4. Export 1080p.

## §3 Post (10 min)

**YouTube (Skoolyng or Tutorcross, per the plan):**
- Title: what happens + who it is for, under 60 characters. Example: *"6-year-old programs a robot with paper tiles"*
- Description: 2 lines + "Skoolyng — hands-on logical thinking for children" + WhatsApp number.
- Thumbnail: the reaction frame, big 3-word text (Canva, 1280×720).
- Tags: skoolyng, logical thinking, kids coding, telugu.

**Instagram:** same clip, same caption, 5 hashtags, post within the same hour.

Tell the coach "posted" with the link → ✅ in `week-plan.md`, +1 in `metrics.md` videos.

## §4 What to check after 2 days

Views, likes, one comment to reply to. Report the numbers to the coach; they go into `metrics.md` notes.
