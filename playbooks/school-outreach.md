# Playbook — school outreach

Goal: 3–4 schools a month visited by bus from December. Jayasri finds and
finalises the schools; Vamsi prepares what is shown there.

## §1 Build the list (30 min)

1. Open a note. Columns: school · area · grades it has · contact name · phone · how we know them · status.
2. Warm first: parents we know, relatives, Yashvi's school network, Botany Balaji contacts.
3. Then nearby Tier-2/3 private schools reachable by one bus. Google Maps: "private school near <area>".
4. Stop at 5. Five with a contact beats twenty without.
5. Tell the coach the five; they go into `week-plan.md` as the shortlist.

## §2 First contact (10 min per school)

WhatsApp or call. Telugu, friendly, short. Ask for 15 minutes with the principal or coordinator.

> Namaste, I am Jayasri from Skoolyng. We run a 45-minute hands-on logical-thinking
> session for children (Grades 1–10) with small robots and puzzle kits — no screens.
> We are visiting a few schools in <area> in December. Could we show it to you for
> 15 minutes? Which day suits you?

- Write their reply and the date in the list the same day (status: no reply · replied · meeting fixed · declined).
- No reply in 3 days → one polite follow-up. Then leave it.
- Meeting fixed → tell the coach the date and which grades the school has. Deck prep starts a week before.

## §3 Confirming a visit (orphanage or school) — the questions

Ask these on the call and write the answers down; Vamsi plans the session from them.

1. Date and time confirmed? (orphanage: **Sat Nov 21**)
2. How many children, and what ages or grades?
3. Which room — tables? floor? power socket?
4. Photos or video allowed? Of the children, or only of the activity? **If no, no filming.**
5. Who is the contact person on the day, and their phone number?
6. Anything they want the session to cover?

## §4 Before the visit (Jayasri's part)

- Handouts printed (one per child + 2 spare) — `deck-prep.md` §3
- Kit packed and counted with Vamsi — `kit-review.md` §2
- Contact person messaged the day before: "we arrive at <time>"
