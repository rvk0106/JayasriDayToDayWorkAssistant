# Playbook — reviewing a kit as a child would

Vamsi builds the kits (Skooly robot, Decode Locker, Magic Box). Jayasri is the
first person outside the build to use them — her confusion is the most useful
data the kit gets before a child sees it.

## §1 Review (30–45 min, no help from Vamsi)

1. Take the kit and only the instructions a child would get. Do not ask Vamsi.
2. Try to do the first activity. Use a timer.
3. Write, as you go: what you did not understand · what broke or stuck · what you wanted to do but could not · what was fun.
4. Note the time it took.
5. Give the notes to Vamsi and the coach — unedited. "It was confusing here" is the finding; a fix is his job.

## §2 Pack and count (15 min, before a visit)

Checklist for the orphanage/school kit (from `Tutorcross-Experiments/goals.md`):
- [ ] 3 Skooly robots — all switch on, batteries charged
- [ ] 2 Decode Lockers — keypads respond, servo moves
- [ ] Tiles — complete set, counted
- [ ] Magic Box
- [ ] Spares: 1 cell, screwdriver, tape
- [ ] Handouts in a folder (`deck-prep.md` §3)
- [ ] Phone charged, generic deck PDF on it
