# Playbook — decks and handouts per grade

Grades are sometimes known before a visit, sometimes only on the day. So:
**prepare for the grades we expect, keep one generic deck ready.**

## §1 Which deck for which grade

| Grades | Session | Deck source (Vamsi's R&D, `Tutorcross-Experiments`) |
|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | Magic Box · "be the robot" (no kit needed) | Magic Box session plan |
| 4–6 | Skooly robot + tiles · Decode Locker | Skooly curriculum, session 1 |
| 7–10 | Decode Locker · algorithms & logical thinking · career + AI in daily life | workshop catalog one-pagers |

Ask the coach "which deck for Grade N?" — the answer comes from this table.

## §2 Preparing a deck (45–60 min, Canva)

1. Open the existing deck for that grade (Vamsi shares the link in `week-plan.md`).
2. Change only: school name, date, grade, number of children.
3. Check every slide: one idea each, big text, a picture of the kit, no paragraph.
4. Export PDF; send to Vamsi on WhatsApp for a 2-minute look.
5. Tell the coach it is ready → ✅ in `week-plan.md`.

## §3 Handouts (30 min)

1. One A4 per child: the activity's puzzle or worksheet from the session plan.
2. Print count = children + 2 spare. Black and white is fine.
3. Put them in a folder with the kit.

## §4 Magic Box question cards (orphanage and Grade 1–10 sessions)

1. One set per team colour per grade: 5 questions, consecutive card numbers, one card per path (`how-to-run.md` in `Tutorcross-Experiments/experiments/decode-locker/`).
2. Grade 1: sight words with a cartoon · Grade 5: general logic/maths · Grade 10: logic, a little science. Plain language; one line per question.
3. Write them in a sheet: colour · grade · number · question · answer. Send to Vamsi — his script makes the cards and keys.
4. Print on card, cut on the dashed lines, 55 × 80 slips in ID pouches with a white backing card. Teacher cards and cheat sheet printed separately, kept face down.
5. Path sheet per team + a pencil.

## §5 Generic fallback

One deck that works for any grade: what Skoolyng is (1 slide), the kits (3
slides, one each), what a session looks like (1 slide), contact (1 slide).
Keep its PDF on the phone always.
