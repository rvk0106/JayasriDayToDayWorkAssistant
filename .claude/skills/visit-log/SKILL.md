# Visit log — same day, 10 minutes

1. Open `visits.md`; copy the template block under `## Visits`, newest first.
2. Ask the questions in the template order, one at a time, and write her words:
   who we met · what we showed · what landed · what did not · objections heard
   · what they asked for · **change before the next visit** · next step + date · paid?
3. If Vamsi is in the session, ask him the "change before the next visit" line
   — that is the line the whole 6 months is for.
4. Next step with a date → a 🗓️ row in `week-plan.md`.
5. "Paid session agreed" → tell Vamsi to tick the milestone in `DailyUpdates/Vamsi/goals.md` (this repo does not edit it).
6. Kit damage or a missing part → one line for Vamsi, flagged ⛔ in today's log.
7. `done` skill: schools visited +1 in `metrics.md`, commit `log(jayasri): visit <school>`.
