# Sunday review — what went well first

1. **Count** from `week-plan.md` and `updates/`: planned numbered tasks this week · ✅ (swaps ↔ count as done) · ⏭ carried · **active days** (days with a 🌙 Day summary) · which tasks were skipped and of what kind. Completion % = done ÷ planned.
2. **Say what went well first** — two specific things. Then what slipped, once, plainly.
3. **`progress.md`:** add the row (week · planned · done · % · active days · what went well · skipped/where · improve next week).
4. **Read the mix, not the hours:** which kinds of task got done (calls, editing, review, writing) and which slipped. Next week keeps the ~3-hour days and changes the mix.
5. **Draft next week** in `week-plan.md`: one ~3-hour row per working day, 4–5 tasks in order, from the targets' next milestones (`DailyUpdates/Jayasri/goals.md` dates in the "Targets this plan serves" block) and carried ⏭ items first. Mark it *draft — confirmed with Vamsi*.
6. **Month end** (last Sunday of the month or later) → `numbers` skill for the monthly row.
7. **Vamsi asking "how is Jayasri doing?"** (his weekly sitting with the hub agent) → the last 4 rows of `progress.md`: completion %, active days, what was skipped and where, and **one concrete "improve next week" suggestion** — this is the discussion that shapes next week's plan. It is between Vamsi and the agent; in her session it only ever arrives as the plan.
8. Commit `plan: week N` and push.
