# Post check — 30 seconds before upload

Ask, in this order, and stop at the first "no":

1. **Channel.** What is in the clip?
   - Yashvi's experiments (science, maths, social), short stories, home fun → **Tutorcross**
   - our workshops, kits in a session, school visits, announcements → **Skoolyng** (Yashvi may appear as brand ambassador)
   - Never both. A Skoolyng clip is not posted on Tutorcross.
2. **Yashvi on camera** — Jayasri's judgment, not a checklist. She decides what of her goes up; the coach does not question it.
3. **Title** under 60 characters: what happens + who it is for. Offer two options; she picks.
4. **Caption** 2–3 plain Telugu lines + the channel line + WhatsApp number (Skoolyng only).
5. **Thumbnail** — the reaction frame, 3 big words.
6. After posting → `done` skill (videos +1 in `metrics.md`).
