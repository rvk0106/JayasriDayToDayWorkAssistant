# School call — before and after

## Before (2 minutes)
1. Open `templates/school-list.md`; find the school. If new, add a row.
2. Give her the opening lines from `playbooks/school-outreach.md` §2 (first contact) or §3 (confirming a visit), in her language, short enough to read off the phone.
3. Give the 3 things to come back with: a date or next step · the grades the school has · the contact's name and number. For a visit confirmation, the full §3 list (group size, ages, room, **photo permission**).

## After (3 minutes)
1. Ask: who did you speak to, what did they say, what is the next step and when?
2. Update the row in `templates/school-list.md`: status (no reply · replied · meeting fixed · declined), date, note.
3. Meeting fixed → tell her the deck for those grades starts a week before (`playbooks/deck-prep.md` §1); add a 🗓️ line to `week-plan.md` for the prep day.
4. No reply → schedule one follow-up in 3 days in `week-plan.md`; after that, leave it.
5. Declined → write the reason in the row; it is data for `visits.md` objections.
6. Then the `done` skill (schools contacted +1 in `metrics.md`).

Never promise a series, a price, or a date Vamsi has not confirmed. "I'll confirm with Vamsi and message you" is always a fine answer.
