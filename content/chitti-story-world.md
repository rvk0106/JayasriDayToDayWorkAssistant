# చిట్టి – టింకు కథలు — the Tutorcross story world

> Decided by Jayasri, 23 Nov 2026, after her YouTube research (`youtube-research.md` — PunToon Kids keeps the
> same characters in every story). Every new Tutorcross story is set here, with these characters. To share with Vamsi.

| Who | Name | Who they are |
|---|---|---|
| 👧 The girl (lead) | **చిట్టి (Chitti)** | a little girl in the village — curious, cheerful |
| 👩 Amma | **కమల (Kamala)** — lotus 🪷 | Chitti's mother |
| 👨 Nanna | **రఘు (Raghu)** (renamed from Ravi, 23 Nov) | Chitti's father, a farmer |
| 🎒 Friend | **బుజ్జి (Bujji)** | the naughty one, always makes everyone laugh |
| 🎒 Friend | **గౌరి (Gowri)** | the clever girl who asks questions |
| 🎒 Friend | **సూరి (Suri)** | a little timid, with a kind heart |
| 🐕 Pet | **టింకు (Tinku)** | Chitti's puppy, in every story |
| 🏡 Village | **ఊటుకూరు (Vutukuru)** — ఊట = a spring of water 💧 | fields, a pond, trees, animals and birds, the village school |

**Series title (Jayasri, 23 Nov, final): చిట్టి – టింకు కథలు** 🐥🐕 — title tail on every video: `| చిట్టి – టింకు కథలు | Telugu Moral Stories | Tutorcross`. Each story ends with a **"చిట్టి చెప్పిన నీతి"** card — Chitti says the moral.

**Story pattern:** a small event at home, at school or in nature → the children solve it → **Chitti says the నీతి** at the end, in Telugu + `Moral: (English)`.

**Character sheet (Canva, 23 Nov):** https://canva.link/y9b6k2dafesz1qi — 7 pages: cover, Chitti (front, side, 3 faces), Tinku (sitting, running), Amma & Nanna, school friends, Vutukuru village, everyone together. As drawn: Chitti — yellow blouse, red skirt, two plaits with red bows · Tinku — cream puppy with brown ears and patches, red collar with a gold bell · Kamala — orange saree · Raghu — white shirt and dhoti, red sash, a sickle · Bujji — yellow shirt, brown shorts · Gowri — green langa · Suri — cream shirt, blue shorts. Reuse these images as the reference for every story.
Known gaps in the sheet: Gowri has no glasses (asked for), and the "everyone together" page shows 7 children, not the family and Tinku.
