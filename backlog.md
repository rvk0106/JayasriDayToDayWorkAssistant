# Backlog — the alternatives pool

> When the day's task does not fit the mood, the coach offers **one** item from
> a different row here. Taken items get a date and move to `week-plan.md`.
> Nothing here has a deadline; nothing here is reported as overdue.

## Calls (short, talking)
- Call 2 nearby activity centres — ask their fees and batch size, note it in `metrics.md`
- Call one school from the list that has not replied
- Ask a parent we know which school their child goes to and who to talk to there

## Editing (quiet, hands-on)
- Cut a 30-second clip from existing experiment footage
- Add Telugu caption + voice-over to a cut clip
- Make a 1080×1080 thumbnail in Canva for the next video

## Writing (thinking)
- Write the intro message for schools in Telugu, 4 lines
- Write one post caption for Instagram from the latest video
- Note 3 questions a parent would ask about the workshop, with our answers

## Review (looking, no making)
- Try the Magic Box / Skooly / Decode Locker as a child would; write what confused you
- Watch the last published video and note one thing to change in the next
- Read one grade's deck and mark anything a Grade 1–3 child would not follow

## Research (browsing)
- Look at 2 similar YouTube channels — what their titles and thumbnails do
- Find 3 schools in a new area with their phone numbers
